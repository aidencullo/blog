I created this site, it's just called "Thread." The idea is that I just create a website where anyone can go on it and comment anonymously. I'm just kind of curious where that goes. I wonder what people write. There's no censorship on it or anything, so I'm sure immediately people will start spamming it or try to expose security flaws or just say profane things. You know, probably have to shut it down immediately if someone posts someone's personally identifiable information. I suppose this is really quite an unsustainable idea or project, but I'm kind of just starting it as a social experiment. I'm sure I'll have to take it down shortly. It'll probably get taken down automatically by some service, but that's kind of the idea.

I don't know, just putting it out there. I think at one point or another this idea pops in my head, and now it's just like, what if we create a social platform like that? What would it be like, how would people react, and maybe it'll grow into something bigger. I'm not sure. Maybe it will grow into a different application.
