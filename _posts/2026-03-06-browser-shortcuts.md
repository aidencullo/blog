Ten Chrome/Firefox shortcuts I keep coming back to:

**Cmd+L** — jump straight to the address bar. Instantly highlights the URL so you can type a new one or search. Huge for keyboard-driven browsing.

**Cmd+M** — minimize the window. Simple but way faster than reaching for the mouse.

**Cmd+Ctrl+F** — enter full screen. No menu bar, no dock — just the page. Press again to exit.

**Cmd+T** — open a new tab.

**Cmd+W** — close the current tab without touching the X.

**Cmd+Shift+T** — reopen the last closed tab. Press repeatedly to step back through your history.

**Cmd+R** — reload the page. Add Shift for a hard reload that bypasses the cache.

**Cmd+F** — find on page. Jump to any word on the current page instantly.

**Cmd+[ / Cmd+]** — go back or forward. Same as the arrow buttons, without moving your hands.

**Cmd+Shift+N** — open a private/incognito window. No cookies, no session, no history.
