I wrote one yesterday. This post is just my current feelings on AI and the future of humanity.

I've been watching videos today, podcasts sometimes — academics, industry leaders, YouTubers, influencers, TED talks, that sort of thing. Yeah, of course I still feel pretty fearful about it. I don't want to be pessimistic, but I see a negative future. I don't feel hopeful. And partially that's just a reflection of how I feel, I'm sure, but I just cannot shake the feeling that it's so hard for us to predict any projection of this technology and our own society, our civilization.

Because, you know, it's obviously unprecedented, and it seems hard for us, in real time, to visualize or even conceptualize exponential growth — both the exponential growth of technology and its capabilities, but also a future where we're not in control.

As I mentioned in my most recent post yesterday, I think people have certain beliefs and then they seek out news, academic research, or just any content on the internet that will reinforce those beliefs. So if you believe that it's all hype, you know, you can find plenty of academics and industry leaders who hold those exact beliefs. Eric Schmidt, for example. Or maybe he's doing the same thing. I have no idea.

But yeah, I mean, I think, you know, if there's a good number of people in these other frontiers who are extremely outspoken about dangerous technologies, it's pretty obvious. Even if we just talk about Hugging Face — I don't know, I'd guess it was a few months ago — so obviously there's probably been some progression in open models since then.

I don't know. I feel like it's somewhat clear to me that there's so much uncertainty. I'm pretty sure we just can't predict the behavior of these models anywhere close to clearly. I don't know if that's the default or the norm for these models, but clearly there have been many instances of difficult behavior, too.

So yeah, still pretty pessimistic about it. I know there are scenarios — like the one I was talking about yesterday — where, if we create this powerful intelligence, it's so sophisticated and self-actualized that maybe you and I seem pathetic and not worth coming back to. So predictable.
